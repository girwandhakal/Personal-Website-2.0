WorthIt: a quick look at your cart before checkout

We built WorthIt at Hacklanta 2026 to catch things you might miss before checking out on Amazon or Walmart, like an item you bought recently or a cheaper store-brand option.

Browser extensions · Hacklanta 2026
Tools: TypeScript, Chrome Manifest V3, WXT, React, Gmail API, IndexedDB

Checking the cart

The Chrome extension can pause the first checkout click and flag up to three items. If something looks like a repeat purchase, it can ask whether you meant to buy it again. You can go straight to checkout whenever you’re ready.

Getting the first review to load

Amazon and Walmart lay out their carts differently, so we wrote an adapter for each. In testing, waiting for every product detail took about four seconds. We opened the review while those details loaded and got the first response down to 0.27 seconds.

Keeping purchase history on the device

Purchase history stays in the browser. If you choose to import Gmail receipts, the extension reads them locally and lets you review what to save. It discards the email bodies.

What we tested

We tested the checkout flow against saved examples of each retailer’s pages. The hackathon build runs as an unpacked Chrome extension. A public release would still need live retailer checks and Gmail verification.
