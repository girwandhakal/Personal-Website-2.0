Projects / Cueframe

Cueframe: a teleprompter for slide decks

I’m building Cueframe to turn a slide deck into a teleprompter script quickly. I wanted to spend my prep time practicing the talk instead of staring at a blank page.

Product engineering · AI systems
Tools: TypeScript, Next.js, OpenAI Responses API, Lexical, IndexedDB, Firebase

Writing the script

I upload a PDF or PowerPoint deck and set a time limit and reading pace. Cueframe uses those settings to work out how much to write for each slide. AI drafts the script in small batches, and I can edit each slide or go back to an earlier draft.

Giving the talk

The script scrolls in my presenter window while the audience sees the slides. I can see how much time I’ve spent on each slide and review my timing afterward. Projects are saved on the device, and cloud sync is optional.

Testing the drafts

I tried adding a second AI pass to review and fix the draft. It won in both reading orders for 13 of 15 completed comparisons, but it pushed the median wait from about 9 to 27 seconds on three-slide decks. When users still pointed out lines that sounded awkward out loud, I dropped the extra pass and worked on the writing instructions.

Trying it with presenters

I’m working toward an invite-only pilot. I want to hear where people stumble when they read a draft out loud. That’s the feedback I need to know what to fix next.
