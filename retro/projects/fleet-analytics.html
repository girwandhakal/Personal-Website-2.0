Projects / Fleet analytics

Fleet analytics: which network devices should we replace first?

At UA Innovate, my team built a dashboard to help Southern Company decide which network devices to replace first. We wanted to put failure risk, support status, and replacement cost in the same view.

Team project · UA Innovate
Tools: Python, Streamlit, Pandas, Plotly, Scikit-learn, GPT-4o

Mapping the equipment

I brought the risk scores into the dashboard and mapped more than 1,000 devices. We added forecasts and a budget simulator so planners could see how their priorities changed under different assumptions.

Giving the assistant the page it needs

I built an assistant called Southern Spark. I gave it a summary of the page the planner was on, with details like risk by state, support status, and replacement cost. That let it answer questions about the patterns on screen.

What the demo could answer

The assistant saw page summaries. To explain why a particular device had a high score, it would need to look up that device’s record.

At the hackathon

We took second place at UA Innovate. The demo let us go from the risk map to a forecast or budget question in the same dashboard.
