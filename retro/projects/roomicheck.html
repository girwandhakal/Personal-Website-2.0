Projects / RoomiCheck

RoomiCheck: asking better roommate questions

We built RoomiCheck for student housing. I wanted students to describe how they live in their own words and get follow-up questions based on what they’d already said.

Product engineering · Co-living assessment
Tools: Python, FastAPI, structured LLM outputs, Next.js

Why follow-ups matter

A student might say they’re fine with guests on weekends and say nothing about overnight visits. The questionnaire can ask about that next. I led engineering and built the FastAPI app that turns those answers into a profile.

How an answer becomes a profile

The AI reads a redacted answer and returns labels with quotes and a confidence estimate. The backend uses those labels to score the profile and choose the next question. It checks eight areas, including sleep, cleanliness, guests, and how students handle conflict.

What the prototype does

The prototype produces a profile someone can review. I can trace a score back to the answer that led to it. We stopped at profiles for this milestone. Matching roommates would take its own tests of how well two profiles predict compatibility.

Trying it with students

We piloted the questionnaire with more than 50 users and compared two interview strategies. We also received $2,500 in funding through RiverPitch and the Aldag Entrepreneurship Competition.
