Alabama Life Research Institute

Machine Learning Researcher
January 2025 – Present
Tuscaloosa, AL

A model score sent me back to the dataset. Two transcripts could look independent in a split and still come from the same child. That changed how I set up held-out experiments and what I look for before sharing a result.

Learn more about my research

Model development

  • I trained and evaluated models on more than 60,000 child utterances to see which communication features were predictive. I was the first author on the paper we submitted for peer review.
  • I fine-tuned FLAN-T5 to use alternative speech-recognition outputs. In an earlier experiment with 90,000 utterances, word error rate fell from 17.2 to 14.4. I track the revised held-out evaluation separately.
  • I compared DeiT, Swin Transformer, and skeleton-based models on seven hours of video to see how they classified gestures.

Data engineering

  • I wrote Python pipelines that bring transcripts, metadata, and audio together, then validate and export the inputs for modeling.
  • I standardized labels, added consistency checks, and made sampling repeatable so groups that contributed more data would have less sway. I kept the validation reports and exclusion logs so I could revisit those choices.

Evaluation and research infrastructure

  • I traced inflated accuracy to the train/test split and rebuilt it so recordings from the same participant stay together. I added regression tests to check that separation.
  • I used word error rates, child-level analysis, and mixed-effects models to compare groups while accounting for repeated observations. I documented where differences in the corpora limited those comparisons.
  • I checked preprocessing changes against actual prediction errors. When a cleaner removed valid speech patterns, I took it out.
  • I set up SLURM jobs to resume training, inference, and evaluation. I kept data and config fingerprints, archived results, and machine-readable reports so I could reproduce a run.
