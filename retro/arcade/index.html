How to chase a high score

Gold packets score 10 points. Collect consecutive packets to build a multiplier up to ×5. Missing one or hitting a bug resets the streak. Survive each wave for another point. The pace picks up as you play.

Red bugs are marked with an ×; gold packets have a +. After a hit, your computer gets a brief shield. Your best score stays in this browser when storage is available. Switching tabs pauses the game.
